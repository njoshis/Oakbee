Understanding Real Raw Honey & The Industrial Deception

Supermarket honey is heavily pasteurized at 70°C and micro-filtered, killing beneficial invertase enzymes and stripping natural pollen.

science Why Supermarket Honey Never Crystallizes:

Industrial processors intentionally strip pollen grains so honey cannot form crystallization nuclei. Real raw honey is biologically alive and naturally crystallizes.

Crystallization is spontaneous physical separation of glucose from supersaturated natural flower nectar. Natural honeys high in glucose (like our Mustard Honey) form fine, velvety crystals within weeks of extraction in colder temperatures. Real raw honey is biologically alive. Place jar in warm water (<40°C) to liquefy if preferred.
Commercial packers buy aggregate drums from unknown sources, ultra-pasteurize them, and filter out all pollen to hide geographical origin and prevent crystallization, rendering medicinal honey into dead commercial sugar syrup.
Rather than confining bees in one spot and feeding them refined sugar when flowers fade, we move our hives overnight following natural floral blooming cycles from high Kumaon forests to winter golden plains.
